## 02-burnout-signs (2026-10-14)

Image: `Lumina-02-burnout-signs.png`

Burnout rarely arrives overnight. Watch for these four signs in your team:

1. People are always tired.
2. Deadlines start slipping.
3. Usually chatty people go quiet in meetings.
4. Sick days increase.

If you notice two or more, it is time to check in. Lumina programmes include weekly wellbeing check-ins so managers can spot problems early.

Save this post for your next team meeting.
